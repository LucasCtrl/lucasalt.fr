# SKG
[SKG](#) (Stacked Keyboard Generator) is an application for my personal usage. 2 years ago, I aquired a CO2 laser cutting machine. At the beginning, I aimed to design and draw mechanical keyboard enclosures but it's time consuming and the general shape is not changing, only the keyboard layout. So, to avoid drawing a keyboard from scratch everytime, I started to develop a generator. The project then evolved into a web application with a 3D viewer allowing everyone to visualize the final product before even starting to cut it on the machine.
