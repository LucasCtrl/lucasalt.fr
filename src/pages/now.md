# SKG
[SKG](#) (Stacked Keyboard Generator) is an application for my personal usage. 2 years ago, I bought a CO2 laser cutting machine. At the beginning, I aimed to design and draw mechanical keyboard enclosures but it's time consuming and the general shape is not changing from keyboard to keyboard. The only parameter that changing the size of it, is the keyboard layout. So, to avoid drawing a keyboard from scratch everytime, I started to develop a generator. The project then evolved into a web application with a 3D viewer allowing everyone to visualize the final product before even starting to cut it on the machine.
